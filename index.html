    How to use MATH-helper

    Everything you can do, in one page.

    🧮 Calculator

    • Type a problem with the keys (or your keyboard) and press the blue =. The notebook shows every step and why.
    • Press ✏️ Show it on paper under a step to see carrying, borrowing, long multiplication or long division.
    • Under the steps, ⚠️ How people get it wrong lists the usual mistakes, so you can avoid them.
    • x and the pink = make equations: 2x + 5 = 17 or x² − 5x + 6 = 0. Press = again to see a second answer.
    • SHIFT gives the gold labels (sin⁻¹, ∛, 10ˣ…). S⇔D switches fraction ↔ decimal. MODE switches degrees/radians.
    • ◀ ▶ move the cursor, DEL deletes, AC clears. On a keyboard: Enter = equals, Backspace, Esc.
    • You can type words too: what is 12 times 7, 5 squared, 17 mod 5, or 2x + 3 > 7. Enter a single number to see its factors.

    ✍️ Draw

    • Write a problem on the graph paper with your mouse, finger or pen.
    • Stop for 10 seconds (watch the timer) and it reads your writing, writes the answer in red and shows the steps. ⚡ Answer now skips the wait.
    • Leave a little space between symbols. Draw fractions with a line, and exponents small and up high. In an equation, ✕ means the letter x.
    • If it misreads something, press 🧮 Fix it on the calculator. Use 🧽 Eraser, ↶ Undo or 🗑 Clear to fix your writing.

    📈 Graph

    • Type equations like y = 2x + 1, y = x² − 4, y = sin(x), x² + y² = 25, y < x + 1 or a point (2, 3). Press Enter for a new line.
    • Drag the paper to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, and ⌂ goes back to the middle. Tap the coloured dot to hide or show a graph.
    • Grey dots show where a graph crosses an axis, turns around, or crosses another graph — tap one to see its coordinates.
    • The notebook explains each graph: slope and intercept, vertex, circle center and radius, plus the distance and midpoint between two points.

    📐 Geometry

    • Pick a shape: rectangles, triangles, Pythagoras, circles, polygons, angles, boxes, cylinders, spheres, cones and pyramids.
    • Type the measurements. It draws the shape to scale and works out area, perimeter, volume, surface area and angles, step by step with each formula.

    📊 Statistics

    • On the calculator, type a list like 2, 4, 4, 5, 9 to get the mean, median, mode and range, or median 5 1 9 3 for just one.

    🏫 Practice

    • Pick a subject and Easy / Medium / Hard. Type your answer on the chalkboard and press Check.
    • 💡 Hint gives a nudge, and 📖 Show me how shows every step. If you make a well-known mistake, it tells you which one.
    • You earn ⭐ stars and 🔥 streaks — 5 in a row rings the bell! Your 📋 Report card shows a grade for each subject.

    🎒 My grade

    • Pick your grade under the sticky note. The examples and Practice subjects focus on what your grade learns. Tap it again to see everything.

    🎵 Music, sound and paper

    • The radio plays study music: ⏮ ⏭ change songs, ▶ pauses, VOL sets the loudness. 🔔 turns sound effects on/off.
    • When a page has more than 10 steps, press ⛶ Full page to make the paper fill the screen (Esc closes it).
    • Your history, stars and grade save by themselves every 30 seconds.
